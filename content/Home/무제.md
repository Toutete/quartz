# CSPR in V_ZBD, ρ (Pilot Power) in CFR Sensing, and Waveform Comparison

Two distinct power ratios in this ISAC system, kept separate:

- **CSPR** — optical carrier-to-signal ratio, set at the MZM (analog optical layer).
- **ρ** — pilot(sensing)/data(comms) power split inside the digital DFT-s-OFDM waveform (baseband/AWG layer). A standard ISAC "power-domain superposition" parameter.

They live at different points of the chain (AWG → MZM) and are independent.

---

## 1. CSPR inside V_ZBD (from the optical field equations)

### 1.1 Optical fields (as given)

$$E_1(t)=E_1,e^{j[2\pi f_1 t+\theta_1(t)]}\quad(\text{LO, unmodulated tone})$$ $$E_2(t)=[E_2+s(t)],e^{j[2\pi f_2 t+\theta_2(t)]}\quad(\text{MZM output})$$

`E_2` = unmodulated optical **carrier** amplitude; `s(t)` = data **sideband**. Thus

$$\boxed{\mathrm{CSPR}=\frac{|E_2|^2}{\langle|s(t)|^2\rangle}}\quad\Rightarrow\quad \frac{s(t)}{E_2}\propto\frac{1}{\sqrt{\mathrm{CSPR}}}.$$

### 1.2 UTC-PD heterodyne → THz

The photomixer beats `E_1` and `E_2`; the THz term is `∝ Re{E_1 E_2^*}`:

$$E_{THz}(t)=E_1[E_2+s(t)],e^{j[\omega_c t+\Delta\theta(t)]},\quad \omega_c=2\pi(f_1-f_2),;\Delta\theta=\theta_1-\theta_2.$$

Amplitude, factoring the carrier:

$$A_{THz}(t)=E_1E_2\Big[1+\tfrac{s(t)}{E_2}\Big]=A_c\Big[1+\tfrac{s(t)}{E_2}\Big], \quad A_c=E_1E_2,;\tfrac{s}{E_2}\propto\tfrac{1}{\sqrt{\mathrm{CSPR}}}.$$

`Δθ = θ_1 − θ_2` is the **common** laser phase — this is what the self-homodyne cancels.

### 1.3 SI and echo carry CSPR

$$V_{SI}(t)=\alpha_{SI}A_c\Big[1+\tfrac{s(t)}{E_2}\Big]e^{j[\omega_c t+\Delta\theta(t)]},$$ $$V_{echo}(t)=\beta_{ec}A_c\Big[1+\tfrac{s(t-\tau)}{E_2}\Big]e^{j[\omega_c(t-\tau)+\Delta\theta(t-\tau)]}.$$

- carrier parts: `α_SI A_c`, `β_ec A_c` (strong when CSPR high)
- sideband parts: `α_SI A_c s/E_2`, `β_ec A_c s(·−τ)/E_2` (data)

### 1.4 ZBD output with CSPR explicit

$$V_{out}=\mathcal R|V_{SI}+V_{echo}|^2.$$

The homodyne cross term (target-bearing), keeping carrier and sideband:

$$2\mathrm{Re}{V_{SI}V_{echo}}=2\alpha_{SI}\beta_{ec}A_c^2, \mathrm{Re}\Big{\big[1+\tfrac{s(t)}{E_2}\big]\big[1+\tfrac{s(t-\tau)}{E_2}\big]^_ e^{j(\omega_c\tau+\Delta\phi)}}$$

- The **carrier×carrier** part (`∝ A_c² = (E_1E_2)²`) is the strong self-homodyne beat → scales with CSPR; it is the SI/target reference used by the CFR.
- The **carrier×sideband** parts (`∝ A_c²·s/E_2 ∝ A_c²/√CSPR`) carry the data/range modulation onto the IF.
- `Δφ = Δθ(t) − Δθ(t−τ)` is the SI–echo phase-noise residual (→0 as τ→0).

**CSPR role:** high CSPR → strong carrier beat → strong homodyne reference (good sensing / good `H_SI`), weak data (poor comms). Low CSPR → opposite. This is the optical-layer sensing↔comms knob, and matches the earlier optimum (≈13 dB for 16-QAM, 13–16 dB for 32-QAM). CSPR sits in the **carrier fraction of the THz field**, i.e. in `A_c` vs `s/E_2`.

## 2. ρ (pilot power ratio): is it a comms thing or ISAC-specific?

### 2.1 The digital waveform (code)

```
tx = √ρ · pilot(ZC)  +  √(1−ρ) · data(SC-FDMA)      # DFT-s-OFDM
```

`ρ` = pilot(sensing) power fraction. **Not** CSPR — this is a baseband/AWG-layer split between a deterministic sensing pilot and random data.

### 2.2 Ordinary comms vs ISAC

- **Ordinary OFDM comms** uses pilots too — but for **channel estimation / sync**, placed sparsely, and one _minimizes_ pilot overhead (pilots are pure overhead for throughput). There is no "sensing power ratio ρ" to tune.
- **ISAC** deliberately **superimposes a deterministic sensing signal** (here a ZC pilot) on the data and tunes its power fraction ρ to trade sensing vs comms. This **power-domain superposition** is an ISAC concept, not a comms necessity. If you only needed comms, you would not raise ρ.

So: **ρ (as a sensing power knob) is ISAC-specific**; pilots-for-channel-estimation exist in plain comms but without the sensing power-allocation role. This is an established ISAC topic (power-domain superposition / superimposed-pilot ISAC; see refs).

## 3. Sensing performance of a ρ-weighted signal in the CFR — processing gain

The pilot is the **deterministic** part; the data part is random and, if used for sensing, injects data-dependent sidelobes. Two ways to read range:

### 3.1 Pilot-only CFR (clean, deterministic)

Use only the ρ-weighted pilot as the reference `S(f)`:

$$\mathrm{SNR}_{sens}\propto \rho,P\cdot G_p,\qquad G_p=T_pB,$$

where `T_p` is the pilot duration and `B` its bandwidth. Compared with using the whole signal, the **processing gain is set by the pilot's** `T_pB`, and the usable power is `ρP`. So raising ρ raises sensing SNR **linearly** but costs comms.

### 3.2 Full-signal CFR (pilot + data)

Divide by the full known `X[k]` (`H=Y/X`). Then all power contributes, but the random data raises the **sidelobe floor** (data-dependent sidelobes). Processing gain is `T·B` of the whole block, but the effective sensing dynamic range is limited by the sidelobes, not noise. Mitigate with pilots/constant-modulus references or mismatched filters.

### 3.3 Effect on the earlier `R_max`

The range limit changes from the pure-noise form to include ρ:

$$\boxed{;\mathrm{SNR}_{sens}(R)=\frac{\rho,G_p,2,\alpha_{SI},\beta_{ec}(R)}{N},\qquad R_{\max}=\Big(\tfrac{2\rho,\alpha_{SI}\sqrt K,G_p}{N,\gamma_{th}}\Big)^{1/2};}$$

- New factor **ρ** (pilot power fraction) multiplies the sensing SNR.
- `G_p = T_pB` uses the **pilot** time-bandwidth product (if pilot-only processing).
- CSPR enters through `α_SI, β_ec` and `A_c` (carrier strength), i.e. via the homodyne reference amplitude — a **separate** lever from ρ.

Two independent sensing knobs: **ρ** (digital pilot fraction) and **CSPR** (optical carrier fraction). Both raise sensing at the cost of comms, but through different physics.

## 4. Updated waveform comparison (ρ as an explicit ISAC axis)

|Criterion|LFM-QAM|OFDM|DFT-s-OFDM|
|---|---|---|---|
|Range method|de-chirp / matched filter|subcarrier channel division + IDFT|channel division + IDFT (X known)|
|Sensing signal|chirp (whole waveform)|superimposed/embedded pilot or full symbol|superimposed ZC pilot (ρ) + data|
|ρ (pilot power) role|n/a — chirp is the sensing signal|tunable (superimposed pilot)|explicit `√ρ·pilot+√(1−ρ)·data`|
|Sensing proc. gain `G_p`|`T·B` (full chirp)|`T·B` (or pilot `T_pB`)|pilot `T_pB` (pilot-only) or `T·B` (full)|
|Sensing SNR scaling|high (all power sensing)|`∝` pilot power|`∝ ρ` (pilot fraction)|
|Sensing sidelobes|excellent (chirp)|data-dependent unless pilot-only|data-dependent; spreading slightly worse|
|Range resolution|c/2B|c/2B|c/2B|
|PAPR (optical cost)|low–moderate|high|**low** (best for photonic-THz)|
|CFO sensitivity (THz)|**low**|high (ICI)|high (ICI)|
|Phase-noise sensitivity|**low**|high|high|
|Comms spectral eff.|moderate (chirp overhead)|high|high|
|Impl. complexity|moderate|moderate|higher (DFT precoder + pilot)|
|Best fit|radar-centric|comms-centric, rich sensing|power-limited photonic ISAC|

### Two independent power knobs in this system

|Knob|Layer|Definition|Raises|Costs|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|**CSPR**|optical (MZM)|`|E_2|²/⟨|
|**ρ**|digital (AWG)|pilot/data power in DFT-s-OFDM|pilot sensing SNR (`∝ρ`), clean CFR|comms data power|

Both trade sensing↔comms; they are set independently and both feed the self-homodyne CFR: CSPR builds the carrier/SI reference amplitude, ρ builds the deterministic pilot the CFR divides by. With SI removed by `H/H_SI−1` (no SI sidelobe), the only caps are comms EVM (upper) and phase-reference/pilot SNR (lower).
